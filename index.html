How they work

The same principles in every app.

Your records stay yours

Everything lives on your devices and syncs through your own private iCloud account. There is no AeroNauticalTrax account and no server holding your history.

No data collected

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers. Nothing about you or your machines is sent to the developer, without your authorization.

Try it, then buy it once per app

Each app is free to try within limits, with a single one-time per app purchase to remove them. No subscriptions, ever. All future updates, minor as well as major, are included in the one time purchase.

Made in the USA

Written, supported and answered from Tucson, Arizona. Not offshored, not outsourced, and not a storefront for someone else's code — one developer, in one place, who answers his own mail.

Built by one person who uses them

Developed and written by a retired airline Captain and current 100T credentialed Merchant Mariner, keeping records on an RV and years of experience in the air and on the water. Write in and you reach the developer, not a queue.
