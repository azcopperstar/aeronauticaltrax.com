What NauticalTrax is not

NauticalTrax is a personal record-keeping tool. The sea service you record here is a working record to help you assemble an application — it is not an official sea service form, and it is not submitted anywhere on your behalf. Nor does it replace a licensed surveyor's report or a yard's work order. Keep the documents your licensing authority and insurer require; use NauticalTrax to have the history and the totals ready when you need them.
