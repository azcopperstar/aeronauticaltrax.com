Sea service

The time you put in counts for something.

Sea service toward a credential is recorded per voyage, not remembered in bulk years later. Every leg you log adds to the totals.

Per-voyage sea service

Waters type, tonnage, capacity served, days of service and hours underway — captured on the voyage rather than reconstructed afterwards.

Credential tracking

Endorsements, issue and expiration dates, and medical certificate expiration, each with its own due date.

Sea service PDF export

One tap for your service log with lifetime totals and current credential status, ready to work from.
