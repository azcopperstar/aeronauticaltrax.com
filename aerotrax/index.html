The aircraft

What an airplane actually needs tracked.

Not a mileage counter with different labels. Aircraft maintenance runs on hours, calendar months and recurring directives at the same time, per component — so that is how AeroTrax models it.

Hangar view

Your whole fleet at a glance — tail number, registration, make and model, specs and photos.

Hobbs & tach time

Both clocks tracked and kept current automatically from flight and fuel entries, rather than typed in twice.

Airworthiness directives

One-time and recurring ADs, each with its own compliance schedule, so a recurring AD comes due like any other interval.

Inspection cycles

Annual, 100-hour, pitot-static, transponder, altimeter and ELT — every cycle on its own clock, calendar or hours.

Component times

Airframe, engine and propeller carry their own total time and time since overhaul, tracked per component.

Flight log

Route, duration, en-route stops, fuel consumed and trip grouping — the aircraft side of every flight you make.

Fuel log

Fill-ups, cost, economy over time and fluid checks, tied to the flights they belong to.

Combined logbook PDF

ADs, inspection status and component times in a single document — the one you hand over when someone asks.

The pilot

Your logbook lives here too.

The aircraft has records that come due. So do you. Keeping both in one app means the flight you just logged updates the airframe time and your night currency at once.

Full flight logbook

PIC, SIC, dual received, solo, night, actual and simulated instrument, cross-country and landings — logged the way the FAA form is laid out.

Certificates & currency

Ratings, medical class and expiration, and a flight review due date calculated for you rather than remembered.

Logbook PDF export

One tap for a complete logbook with lifetime totals and your current certificate status.

What AeroTrax is not

AeroTrax is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not replace the maintenance records your aircraft is required to carry, the judgment or signoff of a certificated mechanic, or any entry a regulation requires you or your mechanic to make. Keep the records you are required to keep — use AeroTrax to know what is coming, and to have it all in one place when someone asks.
