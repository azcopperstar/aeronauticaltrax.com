What it tracks

Built for people who actually keep the records.

Not a mileage counter. VehicleTrax models the way maintenance really works — intervals that come due by odometer, calendar or hours, parts you keep on the shelf, and projects that run for months.

Maintenance schedules

Reusable templates with intervals by mileage or calendar date, so the next service date is always calculated, never remembered.

Fuel & economy

Log fill-ups and watch consumption trend in MPG, L/100 km or km/L — whichever unit you think in.

Specs & parts inventory

Filter numbers, belt sizes, fluid capacities and torque specs stay attached to the vehicle they belong to.

Trip logging

Record trips with distance and cost, so you know what a season of travel actually ran you.

Upgrades with photos

Document every modification with photo attachments and notes — the record a future buyer will want to see.

Projects & punch lists

Plan a long refit as a project, then work it down item by item instead of losing it in a notes app.

Checklists

Reusable checklists with nested sub-items for departure, winterizing, launch or pre-trip inspection.

Expenses & subscriptions

Categorize spending and keep recurring costs — insurance, storage, roadside — in the same ledger.

PDF reports

Generate a clean service history on demand for a buyer, a warranty claim or your own records.

Questions

Before you buy.

I already bought VehicleTrax as a paid app. What happens?
You keep everything. Earlier purchasers are unlocked to the full version at no additional cost — nothing to buy again. If a device ever shows trial limits, tap Restore Purchases in Settings and it will unlock.
What are the trial's limits?
Three things. Two vehicles. Ten records in each table. And two features held back for the full version: exporting, printing or sharing a PDF report, and automatic backups. Everything else is identical — every feature, every screen — so you are evaluating the real app, not a demo. You can still read every report on screen, and manual backup and restore work in the trial, so your records are never locked in. The trial does not expire, and what you enter stays yours when you unlock.
Is my data stored on your servers?
No. Your records live on your devices and sync through your own private iCloud account. There is no VehicleTrax account and the developer collects no data.
Does one purchase cover iPhone, iPad and Mac?
Yes. It is a universal purchase across Apple platforms — buy it once with your Apple Account and it is available on all of your devices, and to your Family Sharing group.
Can I track something that isn't a car?
That is the point. Intervals run on mileage, calendar dates or engine hours, so RVs, boats, trailers, tractors, generators and shop equipment all work the same way a car does.
What if I need help or want a feature?
Write to support and you reach the developer directly, not a queue. Feature requests from owners drive most of what ships.
